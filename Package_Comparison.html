READ THISRent and meals are interchangeable — and that changes the answer

The cap is CNY 45,000/month across all categories, not per category. Laundry takes 1,000, so rent + meals should total 44,000 to fill it. Any split works identically for tax:

meals 15,000 → rent 29,000  ·  meals 12,000 → rent 32,000  ·  meals 10,000 → rent 34,000  ·  meals 5,000 → rent 39,000

But the two are not equally costly to you. Meals are money you spend anyway — claiming them converts existing spending into shelter. Rent above what you actually need is incremental. Fill the quota with meals first and let rent take the remainder: every extra CNY 1,000/month of meal fapiao lets you rent CNY 1,000/month cheaper for an identical tax position. Going from 10,000 to 15,000 of meal claims is worth CNY 60,000/year — the switch above lets you toggle between the two.

The catch is fapiao. Every meal claim needs one, and 15,000/month is CNY 500 a day of receipted spending. That is the real reason meals are "hard to fully utilise" — not the rules, the admin. Assume CNY 10,000 unless you are disciplined about asking for 发票 every time.

CONFIRMEDUBS's ¥33,000,000 is correct — and it is the right number for this comparison

It reconciles to the yen: base ¥23,100,000 + housing allowance ¥6,012,000 + imputed 社宅 benefit ¥3,888,000 = ¥33,000,000. That last component is the market rent on the Ikejiri flat (¥360,000/month) less the ¥36,000 legal rent you pay — real remuneration that never passes through your bank account. Commuting is excluded because it is an expense reimbursement, not pay.

This is standard banking practice: under fixed-versus-variable remuneration rules, housing benefits count as fixed pay. So ¥33,000,000 is your base pay as UBS defines and reports it, even though the payslip shows ¥29,267,760 of cash.

¥33,000,000 vs CNY 1,800,000 is the like-for-like comparison, because both are fixed pay that has to cover housing. That is +32.1%. Use this figure with UBS China HR and with KPMG — it is the one they will recognise. Use net cash (section 1) only for what actually lands in your account.

5  Reconciliation with the UBS non-taxable benefits policy

RESOLVEDThe quota is carved out of base salary, not added to it

The policy is explicit: the five benefits are claimed "as part of Base Salary", housing "as part of their monthly basic salary." There is no additional pot. Gross stays CNY 1,800,000 whatever you claim.

So the quota is worth its tax effect only — but that effect is large, because you sit in the 45% bracket throughout. Every yuan of rent inside the quota costs you 55 fen. That is the single most useful number here, and it is why a compliant lease at a proper rental rate is worth the fapiao paperwork.

RESOLVEDServiced apartments don't qualify — only a proper lease does

Clara Yue confirmed (4 Aug): UBS follows the tax bureau line that only rental agreements qualify for non-taxable housing treatment — serviced apartments bundle services and fall outside the category. That rules out the Grand Hyatt for shelter purposes entirely, which is why it no longer appears as a preset above. Every number in this tool assumes a proper 租赁合同 with a compliant monthly fapiao, not a hotel folio.

An exception route exists on paper, but nobody has obtained written tax-bureau confirmation, and the arithmetic makes it moot anyway — even winning it outright nets barely more than a fully-sheltered CNY 34,000 lease. Rent properly rather than chase it. What any lease needs: original monthly fapiao in your name, rent quoted separately from utilities/management/cleaning fees, and the landlord's ownership certificate and ID.

UNCHANGEDThe policy does not fix the contract gap

• §VIII.1 — UBS "reserves the right to amend or revoke this scheme at any time at its discretion."
• §VIII.2 — UBS "has no responsibility or obligation to arrange employee compensation in the most tax-efficient manner."
• Title — the scheme runs 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2026, four months of a 24-month contract. It notes the underlying IIT regime extends to 31 Dec 2027.

As a pre-existing policy it is what contract Art. 44 excludes and Art. 53(1) supersedes. Committing to CNY 30,000+ rent on the strength of a revocable annual policy is precisely the exposure worth watching — if the scheme were withdrawn, rent jumps to full sticker price overnight.

YOUR POINTUS tax — and no help from UBS

§VII.3 makes you "fully responsible for all taxes, including individual income tax, both inside and outside China." No gross-up, no equalisation.

Confirmed 3 Aug from the 2025 return — the carryforward is $381,368 regular / $450,232 AMT. That is why 2025 AGI of $395,074 produced a total US tax of $1,461.

The zero-US-tax position continues in Beijing. On CNY 1.8M base plus ~CNY 913K bonus, Chinese IIT with the quota runs USD 111,703 — 27.3% effective — against a US MFJ liability of USD 76,084, or 18.6%. China still sits well above US rates, so the credit covers the liability in full and you generate a further ~USD 35,600 of excess credit each year. The balance grows rather than drains, even though the US ignores the Chinese housing exemption — the comparison above already charges full income to the US side.

Which also defuses the housing-quota leak some people worry about: claiming more shelter lowers Chinese tax, but Chinese tax has USD 35,600/year of headroom above the US liability before any residual could arise. The FEIE-versus-credit question settles decisively toward the credit — FEIE for 2026 is only $132,900 and caps out, while the credit does not. The vintage schedule is at Form 1116 Schedule B — tranches of $14,087 / $31,359 / $43,982 / $63,460 / $63,051 / $30,593 / $54,281 / $80,555 from 2018 through 2025, each expiring ten years on. The one real ask for KPMG: whether Form 2555 was ever actually elected in a prior year, because revoking a FEIE election locks you out for five years without IRS consent.

ADDED 3 AUGTwo costs that fall only on the Beijing side

Rieko's social insurance — ¥240,040/year. Her Japanese cover is derivative of yours, not her own. Leaving UBS Japan ends both her 健康保険被扶養者 and 国民年金第3号 status — her staying in Tokyo makes no difference. She moves to 第1号 at ¥215,040/year and enrols in 国民健康保険 at roughly ¥25,000/year, but only if the 7割軽減 is claimed — not automatic. Tied to the Tokyo-household toggle above, since the liability lapses if she does not remain in Japan.

Private medical — resolved 4 August, and the answer is cheap. The plan is Ping An Health Insurance high-end: RMB 2,000,000 limit per person per period, no deductible, no waiting period. Area of coverage is Greater China only, so Japan trips are self-insured via 一時帰国保険 at roughly ¥15,000/trip — ¥60,000/year is the default above. A second full international plan is not warranted. Rieko needs nothing extra; her 国保 covers her at the standard 30% copay.

Open question worth money: which plan tier. The Provider Copayment List includes Beijing United Family, where Core/Downgrade carry 100% coinsurance against 40% on Upgrade Plans 1/2. Beijing United is the default expat hospital, so tier determines whether it's usable — ask HR which tier applies and what an upgrade costs.

CLOSEDJapanese exit tax does not apply

国外転出時課税 needs both limbs: ¥100M+ in covered assets and 5+ of the last 10 years Japan-resident. Your permanent residence meets the residency limb, but covered assets total ¥44.5M — 44.5% of the ¥100M threshold on the most aggressive reading available. Markets would need to rise 2.25x before departure to bring you into scope. No filing, no declaration, no 納税猶予 election required.
